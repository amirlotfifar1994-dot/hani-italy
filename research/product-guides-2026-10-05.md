## Content and sources

Quantities and methods are original editorial home-cooking proposals and are explicitly not kitchen-tested. Each guide distinguishes home cooking from factory ingredients/heating. Sources support specific documented variations, not a claim that all families use the same recipe. Italy adaptations identify ingredient names and flavour differences without asserting local stock. Hani pack photographs remain clearly labelled packaging, not finished recipe results; sample products use labelled conceptual imagery.

Ghormeh Sabzi includes a five-step meatless method, comparison with meat and a documented Tabriz family variation, fresh/dried/frozen/pre-fried herbs, Italian shopping vocabulary, substitutions and five FAQs. Health Canada dry-kidney-bean instructions verified against the official page: at least five hours soaking, discard water, at least ten minutes vigorous boiling; slow cookers are unsuitable for this initial step.
