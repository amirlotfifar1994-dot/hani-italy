## Generation prompt

Generate a premium panoramic photographic website hero for Persia Market, an Iranian grocery brand in Italy. A cinematic editorial fantasy connecting ancient Iran and Rome through food. Extremely wide landscape, ideally 2560 by 1024, 2.5:1. Seamless, plausible perspective across a sunlit travertine terrace: recognizable Persepolis (Takht-e Jamshid) stone stair reliefs and tall double-bull-headed Achaemenid columns toward the center-left; the recognizable Colosseum of Rome with tiered arcades toward the center-right; distant cypress trees and warm hills softly link the two places. Do not morph the monuments into a single building; this is an imaginative cultural panorama, not a documentary location. A beautifully styled low foreground table in the central/lower-right area carries saffron rice, a meatless herb-and-bean Persian stew, pistachios, pomegranates, a clear glass of amber Persian tea, rustic Italian bread, olive oil, tomatoes and basil. No meat, no wine, no brands or labels, no captions, no letters, no arrows, no flags, no people. Rich cream stone, emerald foliage and warm gold, delicate shadows, realistic food textures, luminous natural late-afternoon light, refined luxury food magazine art direction. Leave the leftmost 28 percent calmer and darker around the middle/lower-left with a softly feathered deep emerald atmospheric shadow that supports white website text; keep the upper-left sunlit and natural, no white wash, no hard rectangle or text panel. Landmarks and main food subjects should remain inside the central horizontal band of the image so a wide desktop crop is graceful. On a narrow mobile crop the central-right shows Roman arches and the table, while horizontal panning can discover Persepolis. Uncluttered, sophisticated, high photographic realism, no border or watermark.
