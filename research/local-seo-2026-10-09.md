## Google guidance used

- [Organization structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization) documents `OnlineStore` as an Organization subtype for ecommerce businesses. Only relevant, known properties were added.
- [Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) excludes online-only businesses. A Google Maps business profile or physical listing was not created.
- [Ecommerce structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/include-structured-data-relevant-to-ecommerce) associates physical-store location and opening hours with LocalBusiness data. No physical-store data was fabricated.
- [Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) identify doorway abuse involving substantially similar pages targeting similar searches. Regional information uses one national guide and the existing substantive city guides.

No FAQ rich-result claim, Google ranking guarantee or public Search Console submission is made.
