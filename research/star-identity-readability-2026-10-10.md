# Refined star identity and hero readability

Owner direction: replace Tehran–Milan with Iran–Italy; return to the original star-like identity and refine it; improve hero text without a white panel behind it.

Production identity is now `dist/assets/persia-star-v2.svg`, an original native vector refinement of the existing eight-point geometric mark. Symmetric inner/outer outlines echo Iranian tile patterns. Two connected seed/leaf forms in the centre represent shared food and connection; this is a design interpretation, not a claim of historical symbolism. The simplified favicon is `dist/favicon-star-v2.svg`. Existing and Pasargadae artwork remains available as archived alternatives and is no longer referenced by the active brand.

The mark appears in home, footer, account, administration, journal, product and information pages and the shared organization metadata. The route labels are Persian Iran–Italy, English IRAN–ITALY and Italian IRAN–ITALIA in the hero postcard and footer.

The owner subsequently rejected the pale desktop scene treatment and requested the same mechanism as mobile. Desktop now uses an image-wide dark green gradient strongest at the text edge, fading across the scene, with warm white titles/body and gold small labels. The earlier cream overlay and light glyph halo are superseded. Descriptions retain a narrower column. There is no rectangular text background or opaque text panel. Mobile retains its full-image scene and touch panning, with stronger text shadows.
